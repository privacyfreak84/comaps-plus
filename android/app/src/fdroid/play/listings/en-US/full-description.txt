Tabula is an offline maps app built on OpenStreetMap data. It is a fork of CoMaps, which continues Organic Maps and MAPS.ME, and keeps what CoMaps does: downloadable maps, search, voice navigation for walking, cycling and driving, hiking trails and contour lines, bookmarks and tracks, and a map editor.

What Tabula adds:
• Long car routes in a few seconds instead of minutes. For each downloaded map the phone prepares extra route data once, in the background. The routes were tested against CoMaps' own router.
• Avoid a town or a road on the route you are looking at: tap it on the route and choose Avoid. The route is calculated again straight away, and the avoided places belong to that route only.
• Avoid tolls, motorways, ferries or dirt roads with nothing extra to prepare.

No account and no ads. Maps are downloaded from CoMaps' servers. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
